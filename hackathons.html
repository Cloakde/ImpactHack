The rules that never change

Same setup, every month.

The theme changes. These don't. Each event's Devpost page has the full rules, including eligibility and team size limits.

hackathon.config
entry_fee     = 0
location      = "online"
open_to       = "students, worldwide"
experience    = "none required"
teams         = "solo or team"
ai_tools      = true        # at every step
build         = "anything that works"
# websites, apps, AI tools, games, hardware

submit = [
  "working prototype",
  "source code",
  "short write-up",
  "2–4 min demo video",
]

Judging

How projects are scored.

Typical weights. Each event lists its exact criteria on its Devpost page.

  1. 20%ImpactDoes it solve a meaningful problem for real people?
  2. 20%Technical executionHow well is it designed and built?
  3. 15%InnovationHow original is the idea and the approach?
  4. 15%FunctionalityDo the main features actually work in the demo?
  5. 15%Use of AI & techWhat does the technology let the product do?
  6. 15%PresentationHow clearly does the team explain the problem and the fix?
