format.md

How each event runs

  1. Theme drops

    We announce the month's theme, rules, and prizes on Devpost.

  2. Register & team up

    Sign up solo or with a team. Find teammates in the community if you need them.

  3. Build

    Find a problem that fits the theme and build a working prototype with any tools you like.

  4. Submit

    Share your project write-up, source code, screenshots, and a 2–4 minute demo video.

  5. Judging & winners

    Judges score every submission and winners are announced. Then the next theme drops.
