how_it_works.md

One month. One problem. Ship something real.

Every hackathon follows the same simple loop, so you can jump in any month, whether it's your first project or your fiftieth.

01 · find

Pick a real problem

Something that bugs you, your school, or your community. Each event has a theme to get you started.

02 · build

Build with any tools

Go solo or form a team. Websites, apps, hardware, AI tools. AI assistants are welcome at every step.

03 · ship

Demo what it does

Submit a working prototype and a short demo video showing your project in action.

04 · repeat

Win, learn, come back

Judges pick winners across categories. Then a new theme drops next month.

faq.md

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?

Our hackathons are for students. Exact eligibility (such as age requirements) can vary by event, so check each hackathon's rules page before you register.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Participating in a Refactor the World hackathon is always free.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Our events are beginner-friendly, and plenty of participants build their very first project with us. Bring curiosity and a problem you care about.

Can I use AI tools?

Yes. AI is fully allowed and encouraged, from brainstorming and writing code to designing interfaces. We especially love projects where AI does something genuinely useful for the end user.

Do I need a team?

No. You can compete on your own or with a team. Each event's rules list any team size limits.

What do I need to submit?

Usually a working prototype, a link to your source code, a short project write-up, and a 2–4 minute demo video showing your project in action. Each event lists its exact requirements.

How often are the hackathons?

Every month, each with a new theme. Join one, or join them all.

How can my company or organization get involved?

We'd love to hear from you. Visit our sponsor page to see the ways you can support student builders.

Ready to refactor something?

A new hackathon starts every month. Bring an idea, or find one when you get there.
