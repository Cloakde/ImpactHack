About us

Refactor the World is a nonprofit that runs free, monthly hackathons where students use technology to solve real problems.

mission.md

Our mission

We believe students don't have to wait until graduation to make a difference. With modern tools and AI, a motivated student can build something genuinely useful in a matter of days.

Our job is to give them the reason, the structure, and the community to do it, every single month, at no cost.

why_the_name.md

Why "refactor"?

In programming, refactoring means taking something that works badly and restructuring it until it works well. A lot of the world's systems could use the same treatment.

CHANGELOG.md

Our story so far

Our first hackathon, ImpactHack 2026, challenged students to build technology that creates meaningful impact in their schools, communities, or the world. More than 450 students signed up.

That response made one thing clear: once wasn't enough. So we're making it a monthly habit, under a new name with a bigger goal.

    Our first online hackathon. 450+ participants, 23 prizes.
