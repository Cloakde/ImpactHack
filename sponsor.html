Ways to help

Pick what fits.

  • Prizes

    Back an award such as Best Social Impact or Best Use of AI. The prize carries your name.

  • A whole hackathon

    Cover the cost of one hackathon and be its presenting sponsor.

  • Tools & credits

    Give participants API credits, software licenses or platform access to build with.

    Send people from your team to judge projects, mentor participants or run a workshop.

    Propose a theme drawn from a real need and see what students build for it.
